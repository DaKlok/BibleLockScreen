# Privacy Policy for BibleLockScreen

**Last updated:** October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **BibleLockScreen** ("the Application") handles user data and information.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
BibleLockScreen is designed with privacy in mind. 
* **No Internet Access & No Personal Data Collection:** The Application operates entirely offline and does not require internet access. It does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (PII) such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or location data.
* **Local Data Processing:** All Bible verses, content, and data are stored locally on the device inside embedded local databases.
* **No Tracking or Analytics:** The Application does not use third-party analytics services, tracking SDKs, or advertising networks.

## 2. Permissions & Device Data
The Application may request specific Android permissions necessary strictly for its core functionality:
* **Storage / Photos & Media:** Used solely to allow the user to save generated images to their local device storage or set wallpapers. No files from your device are uploaded or read by us.
* **Notifications / Lock Screen access:** Used to set or display content on the lock screen as requested by the user.

## 3. Third-Party Services
The Application does not transmit any user data to external servers or third-party services. All processes and image generation are handled locally on your device.

## 4. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
