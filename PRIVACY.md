# Privacy Policy for Bible Lock Screen

This app is designed to be private, open-source, and secure.

## 1. Data Collection
**Bible Lock Screen does not collect, store, or share any personal information.** * We do not have accounts or logins.
* We do not use any analytics or tracking SDKs (no Firebase, no AdMob).

## 2. Internet Access
The app requires internet access for the following purposes:
* **Fetching Content:** The app uses Jsoup to retrieve the "Verse of the Day" from public sources (e.g., YouVersion/Bible.com).
* **Images:** The app uses Coil to load background images if applicable.
These requests are direct between your device and the content provider. We do not sit in the middle of these connections.

## 3. Local Storage
The app may save your preferred settings and cached verses locally on your device. This data never leaves your phone.

## 4. Permissions
* **Internet:** Required to fetch daily verses.
* **Notifications/LockScreen:** Required to display the content on your lock screen.

## 5. Contact
If you have questions, feel free to open an issue on the [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/DaKlok/BibleLockScreen).
